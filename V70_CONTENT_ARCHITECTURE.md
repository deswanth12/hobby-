## 3. Dedicated Inner Pages Distribution

Detailed technical specifications live exclusively on their dedicated pages, eliminating duplicate paragraphs across the site:

| Page Route | Dedicated Content Scope | What Is Excluded from Homepage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`/about` (Firm & Leadership)** | Full founding history, detailed Big 4 / Fortune 50 career narrative, institutional values, ethics statement. | Long biographical paragraphs, personal reflections, firm history timelines. |
| **`/services` (Practice Directory)** | Detailed audit methodologies, GST reconciliation checklists, transfer pricing benchmark models, corporate restructuring scopes. | Multi-bullet deliverables, sub-practice checklists, filing timelines. |
| **`/blog` (Insights Listing)** | Complete archive of statutory analyses, category filtering tabs, search bar, author profiles, reading time badges. | Deep archive of past tax alerts and circular summaries. |
| **`/blog/:slug` (Article Detail)** | Full 1,200–2,500 word technical whitepapers, statutory section citations, tables, circular references, related publications. | Complete article bodies are strictly forbidden on listings or homepage. |
| **`/contact` (Consultation Portal)** | Interactive location map, driving directions, regional client intake guidelines, privacy notice, direct scheduling. | Multi-paragraph office arrival guidelines. |

This content distribution ensures the homepage acts as an executive briefing, while inner pages provide complete technical substance for users seeking detailed counsel.
