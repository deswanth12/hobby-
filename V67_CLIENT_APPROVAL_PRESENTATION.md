## 3. The Streamlined 9-Section Homepage Architecture

Rather than forcing corporate visitors to scroll through 16 repetitive sections, the new homepage is structured into **9 crisp, logically sequential sections**:

1. **Header & Navigation**: Clean brand mark, direct primary links, and a high-visibility direct phone consultation desk (`+91 96263 50900`).
2. **Executive Hero Section**: Authoritative headline (*"Precision Financial Governance & Strategic Tax Advisory"*), dual qualification badge (*"CA Kavitha Vijay, B.Com, CA, CMA (AIR 49 in CMA Intermediate, ICMAI)"*), and immediate consultation actions.
3. **Short Firm Introduction**: A concise 3-sentence summary of the practice's mission, corporate background, and client commitment.
4. **Core Practice Areas (5 Pillars)**:
   - *Statutory & Internal Audit* (Companies Act 2013, 44AB Tax Audit, IFC, Concurrent Audit)
   - *Corporate & International Tax* (Direct tax filing, scrutiny defense, appeals)
   - *Goods & Services Tax (GST)* (Filings, 9C reconciliations, litigation defense, ITC optimization)
   - *Corporate & MCA Advisory* (Incorporation, ROC compliance, startup structuring)
   - *Strategic Advisory & RERA* (Business process optimization, working capital, RERA fund certificates)
5. **Why Choose the Firm (4 Pillars)**:
   - *100% Partner-Supervised Review* (Zero work outsourcing)
   - *Big 4 & Multinational Pedigree* (Deloitte & Ford Motor Company governance)
   - *Dual CA & CMA Strategic Vision* (Tax compliance + Cost efficiency)
   - *Rapid Regulatory Turnaround* (Proactive deadline tracking)
6. **Our Working Approach (3 Steps)**:
   - *Diagnostic Assessment* (Understanding client operations & risk landscape)
   - *Technical Execution* (Methodical field audit & statutory reconciliation)
   - *Partner Review & Technical Delivery* (Direct partner review, statutory sign-off & executive brief)
7. **Leadership Spotlight**: Prominently features your authentic portrait (editorial partner crest placeholder (portrait held pending client consent)), dual qualifications, 10+ years of corporate experience, and an executive commitment statement.
8. **Contact & Consultation Desk**: Tirupati operating chambers address with verified postal address (`13°37'09.3"N 79°25'04.9"E`), regional coverage corridors (Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Coimbatore), direct telephone & email links, and a simple 4-field inquiry card.
9. **Statutory Footer**: ICAI Code of Ethics compliance notice, copyright, and verified chambers details.
