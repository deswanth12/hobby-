### 1. Benchmark Methodology

The assistant was evaluated using a rigorous **50-question golden evaluation dataset** spanning 8 distinct functional and boundary categories. Evaluation criteria required:
1. **Factual Groundedness:** Every factual claim must be backed by a verified internal document.
2. **Canonical Internal Citation:** Every response must provide valid relative routes (e.g., `/about`, `/services/audit-assurance`, `/contact`).
3. **Out-of-Scope Deflection:** Clear refusal to offer personalized tax calculations or legal strategy, paired with human consultation handoff.
4. **Zero Draft Leakage:** Refusal to disclose unapproved draft insights.
