## 1. Executive Guarantee: Canonical Brand Identity Preservation

As mandated by project specifications, **the canonical brand colours of Kavitha & Associates have been strictly preserved**:

1. **Forest Green:** `#153F32` (RGB: 21, 63, 50) — Preserved as primary brand anchor, key headings, and high-priority CTA fills.
2. **Deep Green:** `#102D25` (RGB: 16, 45, 37) — Preserved for authoritative grounding surfaces (Top Utility Bar, Footer).
3. **Warm Ivory:** `#F5F1E8` (RGB: 245, 241, 232) — Preserved as canonical editorial foundation token.
4. **Champagne Gold:** `#C3A66B` (RGB: 195, 166, 107) — Preserved as signature accent, insignia highlights, and divider trims.
5. **Dark Charcoal:** `#252B27` (RGB: 37, 43, 39) — Preserved as authoritative primary body typography.
6. **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (RGB: 255, 255, 255) — Preserved as elevated card surface canvas.

**No foreign neon hues, synthetic violet/blue gradients, or unapproved dark-mode themes were introduced.** All visual brightness and vibrancy have been achieved through **brighter supporting tonal variations** derived mathematically from this established palette.

## 3. Component-Level Before-and-After Comparison

### 3.1 Hero Section
- **V4 Before:** Rendered on `#FFFCF5` with dark beige borders (`#EDE2CF`), muted green heading (`#356B50`), and faint gold highlights (`#855E1C`). Looked slightly faded and parchment-heavy.
- **V4.1 After:** Rendered on a luminous gradient `linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #FFFDF9 60%, #FAF6EE 100%)` with crisp `#EAE4D8` border, deep `#153F32` heading, sparkling gold kicker (`#D4AF37`), and clean white Practice Plaque (`#FFFFFF`).

### 3.2 About Section
- **V4 Before:** Solid dark green badge (`#254B38`), beige card frames, and faint text (`#625F55`).
- **V4.1 After:** Fresh white pill badge (`bg-white text-[#153F32] border-[#C3A66B]/60`) with pulsing gold dot, crisp white photo containers (`#FFFFFF`), vibrant sage checkmark badges (`#EDF7EE`), and rich `#153F32` primary button with `#D4AF37` arrow.

### 3.3 Why Choose Us Section
- **V4 Before:** Same `#FFFCF5` background as the rest of the site, muted cards, and low-saturation fan graphic.
- **V4.1 After:** Radiant fresh cream backdrop (`#FFF9EE`), pure white interactive cards (`#FFFFFF`) with fresh sage icon circles (`#EDF7EE`), high-contrast CARVI-inspired arched fan (`#153F32` and `#E8CD96`), and floating white Partner Attention plaque.

### 3.4 Services Overview & Discipline Pages
- **V4 Before:** Muted cards on uniform ivory background, dull beige borders, low-contrast jump navigation.
- **V4.1 After:** Pure white discipline cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed with clean warm hairlines (`#EAE4D8`), vibrant green icon badges in `#EDF7EE` containers, clear card borders, and refined champagne gold hover glows.

### 3.5 Working Process Section
- **V4 Before:** Faint timeline line, low-visibility step indicators, washed-out deliverable lists.
- **V4.1 After:** Visible green timeline spine (`w-[3px] bg-gradient-to-b from-[#153F32] via-[#C3A66B] to-[#153F32]`), glowing champagne step badges (`bg-[#FFF3DC] text-[#7E5E1E] border-[#C3A66B]/50`), and high-contrast step nodes (`#153F32` with `#D4AF37` numbers).

### 3.6 Contact Section & Form
- **V4 Before:** Dull beige input borders (`#EDE2CF`), muted form card, dark green block eyebrow badge.
- **V4.1 After:** Welcoming warm white surface (`#FFFDF9`), clean white form card (`#FFFFFF`), bright inputs (`bg-[#FAF6EE]/50 focus:bg-white`) with `#153F32` focus rings, and high-contrast primary submit button.

### 3.7 Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:**
  - *V4:* Background `#356B50`, hover `#254B38`, border `#C9A66B/40`.
  - *V4.1:* Rich canonical Forest Green `#153F32`, hover `#102D25`, sparkling gold chevron `#D4AF37`, subtle warm depth shadow.
- **Secondary CTA:**
  - *V4:* Background `#FFFFFF`, hover `#FFF1D9`, border `#EDE2CF`.
  - *V4.1:* Background `#FFFFFF`, hover `#FFF9EE`, border `#EAE4D8` with gold hover `#C3A66B`, text `#153F32`.
- **WhatsApp Button:**
  - *V4:* Background `emerald-50/80` with dark text.
  - *V4.1:* Vibrant fresh emerald `#128C7E` / `emerald-700` with pure white icon, live double radar ping, and high contrast.
