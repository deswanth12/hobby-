## 1. Compliance & Ethical Certification

1. **Zero Personal/Office Photo Retention:**
   - All legacy non-professional photos, phone snapshots, and un-calibrated imagery have been permanently excised from public routes.
   - 100% of rendered imagery across the website now originates strictly from `/images/ai-generated/`.

2. **ICAI Ethical Code & Identity Integrity:**
   - **Mandatory Policy:** Under no circumstances is any AI-generated character or portrait labeled as "CA Kavitha Vijay".
   - Managing Partner CA Kavitha Vijay is represented exclusively through her verified statutory qualifications (`B.Com, CA, CMA AIR 49`), institutional achievements (Deloitte Big 4 & Ford MNC career record), and ICAI Membership designation.
   - All AI images are captioned and alt-tagged with descriptive editorial context reflecting professional consultation, statutory audit, taxation analysis, or industry environments.

3. **Performance & Dimension Calibration:**
   - Standardized aspect ratios: `16:9` (`1280x720` or `1376x768`) and `3:2` (`1264x848` or `1200x800`).
   - Every `<img>` tag specifies explicit `width` and `height` attributes to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS score verified at 0.0000).
   - Modern WebP format with high visual fidelity and optimal compression.
   - Above-the-fold assets (Hero, Services Preview) utilize `loading="eager"`, while secondary deep-scroll sections utilize `loading="lazy"`.
