## 4. Content Strategy Rules & Ethical Boundaries

1. **Zero Keyword Stuffing**: Keyword density is maintained below 2.0% across all editorial copy. Content reads naturally as senior professional advisory text.
2. **Strict ICAI Compliance**: Headings, descriptions, and metadata strictly avoid self-laudatory superlatives ("best CA in India", "cheapest audit", "guaranteed tax refund"). All descriptions highlight verified statutory qualifications, experience, and service scopes.
3. **No Low-Quality Doorway Pages**: City targeting is handled contextually through the centralized `/locations` page and canonical practice areas rather than creating thin, duplicate "CA in [City]" pages.
