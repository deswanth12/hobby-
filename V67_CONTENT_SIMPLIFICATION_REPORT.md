## 1. Executive Summary

Client feedback for V6.7 was unequivocal:
> *"The website has too much unnecessary content. Make it simple, rich-looking, and aligned with our actual brochure. Avoid bloated marketing talk."*

Prior iterations of the website (V6.3 and V6.4) accumulated significant "feature creep" — multiple duplicate service cards, repetitive claims of excellence, wordy paragraphs, separate repetitive FAQ accordions, and dense knowledge-bank blocks that diluted the firm's core message.

This report documents the systematic simplification of the firm's web presence from an overwhelming 16-section layout down to a pristine, authoritative **9-section architecture**.

## 3. Detailed Elimination Inventory: What Was Removed & Why

### 1. Removal of Generic Stock Photography Handshakes & City Skylines
- *Previous State*: Stock images of generic Western office towers, magnifying glasses on balance sheets, and smiling models in suits.
- *Reason for Removal*: Devalues local credibility in Tirupati, Chennai, and Bengaluru; feels like an off-the-shelf template.
- *Replacement*: A single, high-resolution authentic portrait of **CA Kavitha Vijay** (editorial partner crest placeholder (portrait held pending client consent)) combined with elegant typography, generous whitespace, and subtle architectural borders.

### 2. Elimination of Discarded Tax Calculators & Knowledge Bank Widgets
- *Previous State*: Embedded draft income-tax slabs and GST rate lookup widgets.
- *Reason for Removal*: Financial calculation engines require ongoing maintenance upon every statutory circular, risk misleading users if tax brackets change mid-year, and distract corporate clients looking for senior partner advisory.
- *Replacement*: Direct consultation channels with the partner.

### 3. Consolidation of Redundant Service Blocks
- *Previous State*: A dedicated "9 Statutory Audits" section immediately followed by a separate "5 Practice Domains" section with 25 bullet points, creating massive vertical scroll fatigue.
- *Reason for Removal*: Visitors were forced to scroll past 6 screens of text just to see who runs the firm.
- *Replacement*: **5 Comprehensive Practice Pillars** where each card concisely summarizes both the audit specialization and compliance scope in 3 lines.

### 4. Elimination of Repetitive "Why Choose Us" Claims
- *Previous State*: 6 bloated cards stating essentially the same thing with minor variations ("We are accurate", "We are timely", "We are experienced", "We care").
- *Reason for Removal*: Redundant marketing jargon violates the client's request for simple, rich presentation.
- *Replacement*: **4 Distinct Factual Pillars**:
  1. *Direct Partner Supervision* (100% partner-led, no junior outsourcing)
  2. *Big 4 & Multinational Rigor* (Deloitte & Ford Motor Company heritage)
  3. *Dual CA & CMA Strategic Vision* (Tax compliance + Cost efficiency)
  4. *Rapid Regulatory Turnaround* (Proactive regulatory compliance tracking)

### 5. Streamlining the Consultation Form
- *Previous State*: 8 required inputs including company turnover, PAN number, and detailed textareas.
- *Reason for Removal*: High friction caused form abandonment.
- *Replacement*: A clean 4-input consultation card (Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Service of Interest) plus direct click-to-call and WhatsApp consultation buttons.
