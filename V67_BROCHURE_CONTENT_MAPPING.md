## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

A recurring issue in corporate website redesigns is "content drift" — where marketing copywriters introduce exaggerated claims, generic placeholder testimonials, or unverified statistics that contradict the firm's official corporate brochure and violate statutory guidelines issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

This document establishes a **strict 1:1 mapping** between the verified source documents of Kavitha & Associates and the content displayed across the proposed V6.7 website prototypes.

Every metric, qualification, practice area, regional corridor, and contact point is grounded in verified documentation.

## 4. Forbidden & Excluded Items (Strict QA Compliance)

To protect the practice from statutory penalties under the ICAI Code of Ethics and prevent false advertising, the following items are strictly banned:

1. **NO Individual Membership Numbers or Unapproved FRNs on Public Landing Pages**: Unless explicitly confirmed and approved by the partner for public display, individual member numbers are excluded from marketing headers to prevent scraping and unverified indexing.
2. **NO Fabricated Testimonials or Fake Client Logos**: The firm brochure contains zero client testimonials; therefore, no fake five-star reviews or unverified corporate logos shall appear.
3. **NO Comparative Pricing Claims or "Cheapest" Superlatives**: Prohibited under ICAI regulations (Advertising Guidelines).
4. **NO Discarded Complex Tax Calculators or Gimmickry**: Complex tax tools that distract from corporate credibility and require ongoing statutory maintenance are excluded.
5. **NO AI-Generated Replacement Portraits**: Only the authentic, verified photograph of CA Kavitha Vijay (editorial partner crest placeholder (portrait held pending client consent)) is used.
