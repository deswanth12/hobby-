## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

The V6.3 refinement program was commissioned to transform the digital identity of Kavitha & Associates from a photographic-heavy brochure layout into a restrained, high-conviction **minimalist luxury corporate experience**. 

Prior iterations accumulated 19 AI-generated and stock photographs across the homepage and inner routes. While photorealistic, this density inadvertently diluted the firm's core institutional message: statutory rigor, Big 4 analytical standards, dual CA+CMA precision, and direct senior partner accountability.

### Strategic Realignment
- **Photography Reduction:** Reduced total active public photographs from **19 to exactly 5** across the entire digital estate (a 73.7% reduction).
- **Client Portrait Status:** The client portrait of CA Kavitha Vijay remains safely stored in local project assets (`/public/images/partner/ca-kavitha-vijay.webp`), but **strictly unpublished** on all public routes until explicit client written permission is granted. An authoritative gold ICAI practice credential seal medallion is rendered in its place.
- **Architectural Detailing:** Engineered a reusable system of hairline rules, corner brackets, precision dot matrices, orbital contours, and numbered editorial markers.
- **Typography & Whitespace:** Elevated editorial typographic hierarchy using Cormorant Garamond, Newsreader serif display, and Plus Jakarta Sans body copy set on warm ivory (`#FFFDF7`), cream (`#F8EFDF`), and deep botanical forest green (`#153F32`).
- **Zero Regression:** All 23 statutory tax and audit verification tests and all 187 end-to-end multi-tier test cases pass with 100% success. Zero remote deployment was executed.
